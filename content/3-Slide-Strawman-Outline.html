Slide 1 — A Provocative POV on AI in HR

"AI is forcing HR to rewire — not just digitize."

Earn 30 minutes by leading with a thesis, not a pitch.

Strategic intent: Open with a point of view that earns 30 minutes of attention. Establish that we are not selling tech — we are reframing the operating model. Position both firms as having a thesis.

The Headline Tension

HR is now one of the largest functional headcount blocks in most enterprises, and the function with the most uneven AI deployment. Pockets of progress, no unifying logic.

(Marinus tour insight + Silicon Valley reflection.)

Why Now

Clients are moving from "what does AI mean for us?" to "what do we actually do Monday morning?" The conversation must land tactically — fast.

The Provocation — pick one per audience

CHRO

The 3-legged HR stool (CoE / HRBP / Shared Services) collapses to 2. AI absorbs much of what CoE has done; HRBPs become the integrated front line. (Marinus's Dave Ulrich provocation slide.)

Total Rewards

Total Rewards is the natural anchor — comp, benefits, recognition all sit on data AI can reason over. We've fielded ~6 "what should TR look like in the future?" requests in the past few weeks. Start here, scale out.

CIO

Workforce enablement is the "squishy middle" between strategy and tech implementation that nobody is addressing. That's where deployment fails. (Ex-Deloitte consulting head endorsement.)

Suggested visual: Single full-bleed conceptual graphic — either (a) the 2-pillar HR model from Marinus's deck, or (b) an "HR as % of headcount + emerging skills" data callout. Minimal text, one pull-quote.
Slot for: A single proof point — e.g., the Bahamian insurer CIO quote from Miami tour ("best session I've ever had on this topic").

Joint-Only — Why This Pairing

We are the only pairing that can take a client from sensitivity analysis → reimagined jobs → tech stack action plan → operating model change in one continuous arc.

Together we resolve the "scaffolding gap" — most change efforts stop at change management; we change the systemic HR architecture (jobs, structures, comp, data) underneath.
